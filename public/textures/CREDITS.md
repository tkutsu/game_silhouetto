# Texture credits

All textures are CC0 (public domain). Each folder holds WebP `color` and `normal` (OpenGL convention) maps at 384 px, a 256 px `rough` map, and `metal` where metalness varies. Pizza maps are cropped to the inner 70% to drop the plate background. One texture per family, so every part of a shape looks distinct.
